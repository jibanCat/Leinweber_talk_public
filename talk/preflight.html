By hand, on the projector:

  • This page's background is the talk's paper, #F4F2ED: on the projector it should read as warm off-white paper, not grey or blue.
  • Night Shift, True Tone, f.lux, Stage Manager and notifications off; the display not mirrored at a scaled resolution.
  • Measure performance with this window full screen on the projector (F11 / ⌃⌘F), at the resolution you will present at.
  • Full screen in the talk: F.
  • Press S once in the talk: the stills must appear, and S again returns to live rendering.
  • Contrast: open “See the box” both ways from the back of the room. If the web inside the box is hard to make out with standard, choose projector-safe (only the volume's ink changes).
  • The look: the slides are dark; the light version (?look=paper) is for a weak projector. From the back of the room, the map’s points must read on the dark.
